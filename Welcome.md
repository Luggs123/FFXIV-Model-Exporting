# FF14 Model Exporting

Welcome! This GitHub is meant to provide you the knowledge and tools to export your Warrior of Light from Final Fantasy XIV into a 3D model. If you intend to use your Warrior of Light for any of the following, this is for you:
- VRChat Models
- VTuber Models
- 3D Printing
- Animation
- Archival

Users much more experienced than I with the tools used in this process are free to contribute by providing their own guides or amending existing ones by submitting Pull Requests - this is intended to be a collaborative effort. And while I can't guarantee I have the expertise to solve problems that might arise, you are free to submit Issues!
 
It is also for that reason that one of the objectives of this guide is to be as detailed as possible, and directing you in a step-by-step process. Everyone starts from somewhere, and thus I will assume minimal knowledge of the software involved. For any contributors, I hope you too will uphold this standard.

Anyone seeking to enlist or provide help is welcome to join the [help discord](https://discord.gg/uWBuqRUURx)!

Your journey will begin by [bringing your Warrior of Light to Blender](<FFXIV to Blender>).
